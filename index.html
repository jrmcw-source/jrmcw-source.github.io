Job-Cost Ledger — Documented Sample

Job-cost paperwork from oilfield service work, rebuilt as a working ledger and an interactive dashboard: 5,116 line items · $1,433,884 across 92 projects · Oct 2011 – Dec 2014. The second page lists the ten largest projects to the cent and runs the timeline month by month — the bulk of the work landed in 2013. 99.78% of the lines carry a full paper trail: purchase order, vendor invoice, and delivery ticket.
