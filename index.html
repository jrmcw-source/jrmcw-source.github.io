Seventeen years running field inventory, billing, and compliance out of databases I built myself — for oilfield service operations where a lost sack or a wrong line on an invoice was real money. I do the same work now as data analysis: records cleaned, reconciled to the penny, and shown in dashboards a person can judge at a glance.

This is the front room. The work is below; the rest of the house is in the menu — Bio, Contact Info, Links, Pics, Music, and a Junk Drawer.
