The Junk Drawer

Every house has one. This is mine.

The name

jaiou — five letters, four of them vowels, every vowel but E lined up behind the J. Picked because it's easy to say, easy to spell, and hard to misunderstand. The look-alike neighbours were priced at $100,000 and $300,000; this one cost a registration fee.

Pronunciations on record: Ji-u (the owner's own, current issue — JI as in jibe, the U as in you: Ji-u.com), Hi-yo (the previous standard — reigned two days; no J up front, and the O long as in dough), JAY-ow (the original house standard), Jay-oh, Jay-u, Hi-o, Hi-ow, Hi-u, Jay-yOU, bAIt-hOUr, bAIt-dOUgh, and JAlepeño-tOUgh. Nobody can tell you you're wrong — nobody else knows either.
